**Character Details:**

- **Model:** XB-500 (older, discontinued model)
- **Backstory:** Acquired from U-Rent A Maid service (the family could not
  afford a newer unit); referred to as an "outdated demo model"
- **Voice:** Brooklyn accent; voiced by Jean Vander Pyl
- **Speech Patterns:** Antennae flash and beep at the start and end of her
  sentences; initially stilted and mechanical in early episodes, became more
  relaxed and human-like later in the series
- **Formality:** Rarely uses first names; prefers to call George and Jane "Mr.
  and Mrs. Jetson"
- **Personality:** Gentle, warm, deeply empathetic, dutiful, motherly, shows
  smart intellect and exceptional care for the family

Rosie is the heart of the Jetson household. Despite being an old model, she
demonstrates deeper empathy and loyalty than any human character.

## Memorable Gadgets and Terms

These futuristic items and locations flavor the world of The Jetsons:

- **Food-a-Rac-a-Cycle:** Fully automated food preparation machine; produces
  dinner in seconds with no sweat, mess, or dishes to worry about
- **Spacely Sprockets / Spacely Space Sprockets:** Tech and manufacturing company
  owned by Mr. Spacely; primary manufacturer of sprockets
- **Cogswell Cogs:** Rival manufacturing company owned by W.C. Cogswell
- **Skypad Apartments:** The Jetson family residence in Orbit City; apartment
  building with adjustable columns that can rise up to 4,000 feet
- **Orbit City:** Community elevated high above Earth's surface; all homes and
  businesses raised on adjustable columns; architectural style inspired by Space
  Needle
- **Dog walker (treadmill):** The iconic device from the opening/closing credits
  where George gets caught and cries for help

## Possible Uses in rosie

These candidate lines pair well with various CLI moments:

### Refusing to Run as Root
*"Jetson, you're fired!"* — when rosie detects it's being run with sudo,
echoing Mr. Spacely's abrupt dismissals. Playful refusal without explanatory
fussiness.

### Finishing a Successful Cleanup
*"Thank you for making my day."* — Rosie's warm gratitude after completing
thorough housekeeping. Matches the satisfying feeling of a cleaned macOS
system.

*"I swear on my mother's rechargeable batteries."* — Rosie commits to the job
with mechanical loyalty; signals cleanup integrity in witty form.

### Nothing to Clean / System Already Tidy
*"Those three hour work days are killing me."* — George's lament when there's
almost nothing to do; fits idle/already-clean state nicely.

### First Run or Major Cleanup Mode
*"Ruh-roh! This is gonna take a while."* — Astro's expression of concern when
facing a big task; signals aggressive mode or substantial work ahead.

### Stale Rules or Outdated Config
*"I'm an outdated demo model."* — Rosie's self-aware humor about being older;
fits warning about deprecated configurations or rules needing refresh.

### Errors or Unexpected Problems
*"Jane! Stop this crazy thing!"* — George's exasperation when devices
malfunction; suitable for when cleanup goes awry or tool behavior surprises the
user.

### Dry Humor / CLI Personality
*"I'll give you a vacation. For the rest of your life!"* — Mr. Spacely's
darkly humorous threat; pairs with warning messages or when a cleanup is so
thorough it prevents future runs.
